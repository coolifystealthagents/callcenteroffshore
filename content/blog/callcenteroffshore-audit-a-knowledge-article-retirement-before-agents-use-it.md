# Audit a knowledge article retirement before agents use it

## What this workflow decides

Call Center Offshore uses knowledge article retirement to decide what an offshore agent may prepare and what the knowledge owner must decide. The process starts when a knowledge article is scheduled for retirement, not when a supervisor notices the queue is late. The offshore agent records the facts and routes the case; the knowledge owner keeps the decision that changes a customer promise, access, or staffing. That boundary keeps the queue moving without handing judgment to the person closest to the customer.

The first question is not how fast the case closed but whether knowledge article retirement left a record another shift can use. Call Center Offshore asks for the request, the affected queue, the person who asked, and the evidence attached. A case without those fields stays open even when the status says done. The aim is a reviewable path, not a cleaner dashboard.

## The record the shift needs

A useful knowledge article retirement record answers who asked, what changed, which source was checked, and who owns the next action. Record the article and effective version, the replacement or escalation path, and the agent acknowledgement and retirement date with the time each was observed. Mark a missing value as missing instead of filling it from memory. The next reviewer will treat an invented value as fact, and that is how a small gap becomes a customer problem.

Call Center Offshore keeps the original contact and the correction note together for knowledge article retirement. The record should support the decision without a private message thread or a second system login. A reviewer who has to reconstruct the source loses time and may accept a weaker case. The standard is not a perfect file; it is a file that makes the decision reviewable.

## Where the agent role stops

The offshore agent can prepare knowledge article retirement, gather the call context, and draft the note. It cannot approve a refund, change account access, or decide that an exception is safe. The knowledge owner owns those decisions, and the boundary should be written next to the queue. A verbal rule disappears when volume rises, so the stop conditions need to be explicit.

When knowledge article retirement touches money, privacy, legal exposure, or a customer commitment, the dependent action waits for the knowledge owner. Call Center Offshore records the pause reason and the time the issue was raised. The offshore agent routes the question instead of resolving it. That is not delay; it is the control working as designed.

## The daily queue routine

The daily routine for knowledge article retirement is short and repeatable. Start with the oldest open case, then match each item to its contact record. Next, record every missing item and an article retired with no replacement path, then route the case to the knowledge owner. End the shift with a handoff that names what moved and what is still open.

Call Center Offshore keeps the knowledge article retirement queue small enough to finish. A queue that never empties hides its oldest item behind a healthy average. Each case gets one owner, one next action, and one review time. The next person should be able to continue without reconstructing the day.

## Exceptions that must pause

Some knowledge article retirement cases must stop rather than proceed. A missing source, two records that disagree, an expired approval, or an unavailable owner is enough to pause the case. Call Center Offshore records the stop reason and the knowledge owner who can release it. The offshore agent does not guess what the knowledge owner would prefer.

When a knowledge article retirement exception is resolved, the note should say what changed and what would reopen it. A close that says only reviewed is not evidence. Call Center Offshore asks for the destination field that actually changed and the time it changed. Treat an article retired with no replacement path as a stop condition until the knowledge owner confirms the right treatment.

## How to verify and close

Verification for knowledge article retirement means checking that each case has a source, a business purpose, and an approval. A sent message or a closed ticket proves activity, not outcome. Call Center Offshore reviews a small sample against the same definition each month. The sample should include one difficult case, not only the easy queue.

Call Center Offshore verifies knowledge article retirement with the same questions every time. Did the record show the article and effective version and the agent acknowledgement and retirement date? Did the knowledge owner make the decision to retire, replace, or hold the article? If either answer is no, the case is not closed, even when the customer has already been told it is.

## The weekly review and handoff

The weekly handoff for knowledge article retirement belongs with the record, not in a private message. Name what moved, what stalled, and who owns the next check. A Monday reviewer should be able to continue without an interview. Keep the handoff to one page with the open cases and the knowledge article retirement exceptions.

Once a month, pull a small knowledge article retirement sample and test the source, the decision, the execution, and the confirmation. Report the sample size and the exceptions, not only a percentage. The review should end with one process change and one owner. That rhythm turns a recurring queue complaint into a managed control.
