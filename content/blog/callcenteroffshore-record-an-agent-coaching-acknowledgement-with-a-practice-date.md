# Record an agent coaching acknowledgement with a practice date

## What this workflow decides

Call Center Offshore uses coaching acknowledgement to decide what an offshore agent may prepare and what the quality lead must decide. The process starts when a coaching session ends with an action, not when a supervisor notices the queue is late. The offshore agent records the facts and routes the case; the quality lead keeps the decision that changes a customer promise, access, or staffing. That boundary keeps the queue moving without handing judgment to the person closest to the customer.

The first question is not how fast the case closed but whether coaching acknowledgement left a record another shift can use. Call Center Offshore asks for the request, the affected queue, the person who asked, and the evidence attached. A case without those fields stays open even when the status says done. The aim is a reviewable path, not a cleaner dashboard.

## The record the shift needs

A useful coaching acknowledgement record answers who asked, what changed, which source was checked, and who owns the next action. Record the call and scorecard evidence, the agreed practice behavior, and the acknowledgement and practice date with the time each was observed. Mark a missing value as missing instead of filling it from memory. The next reviewer will treat an invented value as fact, and that is how a small gap becomes a customer problem.

Call Center Offshore keeps the original contact and the correction note together for coaching acknowledgement. The record should support the decision without a private message thread or a second system login. A reviewer who has to reconstruct the source loses time and may accept a weaker case. The standard is not a perfect file; it is a file that makes the decision reviewable.

## Where the agent role stops

The offshore agent can prepare coaching acknowledgement, gather the call context, and draft the note. It cannot approve a refund, change account access, or decide that an exception is safe. The quality lead owns those decisions, and the boundary should be written next to the queue. A verbal rule disappears when volume rises, so the stop conditions need to be explicit.

When coaching acknowledgement touches money, privacy, legal exposure, or a customer commitment, the dependent action waits for the quality lead. Call Center Offshore records the pause reason and the time the issue was raised. The offshore agent routes the question instead of resolving it. That is not delay; it is the control working as designed.

## The daily queue routine

The daily routine for coaching acknowledgement is short and repeatable. Start with the oldest open case, then match each item to its contact record. Next, record every missing item and an action with no practice date, then route the case to the quality lead. End the shift with a handoff that names what moved and what is still open.

Call Center Offshore keeps the coaching acknowledgement queue small enough to finish. A queue that never empties hides its oldest item behind a healthy average. Each case gets one owner, one next action, and one review time. The next person should be able to continue without reconstructing the day.

## Exceptions that must pause

Some coaching acknowledgement cases must stop rather than proceed. A missing source, two records that disagree, an expired approval, or an unavailable owner is enough to pause the case. Call Center Offshore records the stop reason and the quality lead who can release it. The offshore agent does not guess what the quality lead would prefer.

When a coaching acknowledgement exception is resolved, the note should say what changed and what would reopen it. A close that says only reviewed is not evidence. Call Center Offshore asks for the destination field that actually changed and the time it changed. Treat an action with no practice date as a stop condition until the quality lead confirms the right treatment.

## How to verify and close

Verification for coaching acknowledgement means checking that each case has a source, a business purpose, and an approval. A sent message or a closed ticket proves activity, not outcome. Call Center Offshore reviews a small sample against the same definition each month. The sample should include one difficult case, not only the easy queue.

Call Center Offshore verifies coaching acknowledgement with the same questions every time. Did the record show the call and scorecard evidence and the acknowledgement and practice date? Did the quality lead make the decision to close, revise, or escalate the coaching? If either answer is no, the case is not closed, even when the customer has already been told it is.

## The weekly review and handoff

The weekly handoff for coaching acknowledgement belongs with the record, not in a private message. Name what moved, what stalled, and who owns the next check. A Monday reviewer should be able to continue without an interview. Keep the handoff to one page with the open cases and the coaching acknowledgement exceptions.

Once a month, pull a small coaching acknowledgement sample and test the source, the decision, the execution, and the confirmation. Report the sample size and the exceptions, not only a percentage. The review should end with one process change and one owner. That rhythm turns a recurring queue complaint into a managed control.
