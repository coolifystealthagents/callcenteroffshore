# Consent Checks Before Adding People to a Support Call

**Published: October 12, 2026**

Consent Checks Before Adding People to a Support Call matters because a third party joins before the customer understands who will hear information or what authority they have. A workable control lets the agent help without converting uncertainty into a claim. It also gives the client a record that can be reviewed after the interaction instead of relying on memory or a disposition label.
