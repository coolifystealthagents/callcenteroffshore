# Recover After-Call Work Before Timeout Loss

**Published: October 13, 2026**

Recover After-Call Work Before Timeout Loss matters when after-call work recovery changes what a customer hears, what an agent records, and what a client may safely conclude. The control is not a polished disposition; it is a traceable outcome that separates verified events from interpretation, preserves uncertainty, and gives the next decision to a named owner. The working artifact is a draft-disposition recovery log, maintained by the platform owner.
