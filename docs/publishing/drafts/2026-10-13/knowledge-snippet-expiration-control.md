# Expire Temporary Knowledge Snippets Safely

**Published: October 13, 2026**

Expire Temporary Knowledge Snippets Safely matters when temporary knowledge expiration changes what a customer hears, what an agent records, and what a client may safely conclude. The control is not a polished disposition; it is a traceable outcome that separates verified events from interpretation, preserves uncertainty, and gives the next decision to a named owner. The working artifact is a snippet register with sunset trigger, maintained by the knowledge manager.
